## Model outputs in `results/`

The prediction files contain text generated by third-party models. Model outputs in `results/` and the annotations in `docs/annotation/` are released under CC BY-SA 4.0, except the
Tiny Aya outputs (`results/bench/tiny-aya-global-q8_0/`, `results/minitest/gpu/preds_*_tiny-aya-global.jsonl`), which
are released under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 because the model's licence (CC BY-NC 4.0) restricts commercial use. Outputs of
hosted models are also released under CC BY-SA 4.0; users must still follow each provider's terms.

- **Tiny Aya** (Cohere, `tiny-aya-global`) is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0 (with Cohere's acceptable use policy); hence the
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence of its outputs.
- Outputs of other open-weight models (e.g. Llama, Gemma, Qwen) are subject to those models' licences.

## Attribution

When you use the data, cite the paper (see the BibTeX entry in `../README.md`) and keep the following
attributions.

- **Wikipedia.** Passages in `data/wiki/passages.jsonl` and the `wiki_excerpt` rows of
  `data/benchmark/template_passages.jsonl` are excerpts of Vietnamese Wikipedia articles (vi.wikipedia.org), licensed
  CC BY-SA 4.0. Every passage keeps its page `title` and `revision_id`. The `wiki_excerpt` rows also keep
  `derived_from`, the source passage id. The authors of a revision are listed in its page history:
  `https://vi.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=<revision_id>`. The excerpts were cut to the lead and one
  administrative-divisions line, and wiki markup was removed. Text was NFC-normalised.
- **National Statistics Office (NSO) conversion table.** The old-to-new ward pairs come from *Bảng chuyển đổi ĐVHC
  mới – cũ*, published by Cục Thống kê (National Statistics Office, Ministry of Finance) at
  <https://danhmuchanhchinh.nso.gov.vn/>. It is an official statistical classification and states no licence, so this
  repository redistributes no source file that contains NSO data or data from a government portal: the table, its copy in
  VietnamProvinces, the canonical tables derived from it, the vietnamadminunits data files (GSO commune and district
  lists, scrapes of sapnhap.bando.com.vn and cas.so, and the tables built from them), their copy in
  undertheseanlp/address, vietaddr's `legacy-map.json` and `vn-units.json` (generated from the NSO table through
  provinces.open-api.vn) and the hoatieu.vn pages of the resolutions. `scripts/fetch_sources.sh` downloads all of them
  from their sources and checks them against the SHA-256 checksums of the files used for the paper.
- **Legal texts.** Province-level groupings follow Resolution 202/2025/QH15 of the National Assembly (12/6/2025).
  Ward-level checks use the 34 Standing Committee resolutions NQ 1654–1687/NQ-UBTVQH15 (16/6/2025). Under Article 15
  of the Law on Intellectual Property, legal normative documents are not protected by copyright.
- **vietnamadminunits** (Tran Ngoc Minh Hieu, MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2025 Tran Ngoc Minh Hieu,
  <https://github.com/tranngocminhhieu/vietnamadminunits>). It was used as the independent cross-check lineage. Its
  copies of the pre-reform GSO registry lists, the cas.so list of new communes and the sapnhap.bando.com.vn
  composition texts are inputs of `data/processed/` (downloaded, not redistributed). The MIT notice is kept in `data/raw/vietnamadminunits/SOURCE.md`.
- **Synthetic content.** House numbers and hamlet names in T4 addresses are synthetic. They refer to no real person.
