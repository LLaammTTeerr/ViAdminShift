# UBTVQH15 ward-level resolutions NQ 1654–1687/NQ-UBTVQH15 (16/6/2025) — text reproductions

- What: the 34 resolutions of the Standing Committee of the National Assembly
  ("Nghị quyết về việc sắp xếp các đơn vị hành chính cấp xã của tỉnh/thành phố ...", 16/6/2025),
  one per new province. Điều 1 lists, clause by clause ("khoản"), which old communes form each new one.
- Publisher of these copies: hoatieu.vn (third-party legal-text site). These are HTML reproductions,
  NOT the official scanned PDFs (vanban.chinhphu.vn), which could not be OCRed here. Page titles were
  checked to carry the right resolution number and province.
- Retrieved with curl (user-agent "Mozilla/5.0"), saved untouched as `html/<number>.html`.
- License: legal normative documents are not protected by copyright (Luật Sở hữu trí tuệ, Điều 15);
  the HTML wrapper/layout is hoatieu.vn's. Used for research verification only. *Not redistributed*:
  `scripts/fetch_sources.sh` downloads the pages and checks the parsed Điều 1 text against the version used for the
  paper (the HTML itself changes between downloads; `SHA256SUMS` lists the files used for the paper).
- Parsed as data only by `src/viadmin/data/resolutions.py` (stdlib HTMLParser; scripts are never run).
  The reproduction glues some words together ("Phong Quangthành", "Anvà"); the parser repairs the
  common cases. Used by the build to (a) choose the resolution's spelling of old ward names and
  (b) document pairs added from the resolution (see docs/data_report.md).
